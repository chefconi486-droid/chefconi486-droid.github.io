Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy describes how the "GTA6 Kanal Uploader" application ("the App"), operated by the Wasted Daily YouTube channel, handles information. The App is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Wasted Daily channel.

What the App does

The App uploads videos to, and reads basic information about, the Wasted Daily YouTube channel using the YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's YouTube account.

Information accessed

When the channel owner authorizes the App, it receives an OAuth access token for the owner's YouTube account with the scopes needed to upload videos and read channel and video details (title, description, statistics, upload status). The App does not access email, contacts, or any data from other Google services.

How information is used and stored

Channel and video information is used only to upload videos and to confirm that uploads succeeded. OAuth tokens are stored by our integration provider (Composio) solely to make these API requests on the channel owner's behalf. The App does not sell, rent, or share data with third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the App can no longer access the channel, and stored tokens become invalid.
